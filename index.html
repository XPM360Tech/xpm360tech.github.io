WINDOWS / PERSONAL MUSIC ORGANIZER

NorVil Tech - Personal Music Organizer

Identify tracks, edit tags and album art, organize and rename files in batches, review duplicates safely, and undo changes. Designed for personal music collections and local audio files.

A SAFER WAY TO ORGANIZE YOUR MUSIC LIBRARY

NorVil Tech - Personal Music Organizer

  • Identify tracks using acoustic fingerprint lookups, including when tags are missing.
  • Rename files with presets or custom patterns and organize them into folders.
  • Edit audio tags and look up album artwork.
  • Review duplicates without automatically deleting files; undo the last batch operation.
  • The free edition processes up to 25 songs per batch. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited batch processing.
  • Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Supports MP3, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, WAV, WMA, and OPUS.
  • Internet is needed for track and artwork lookups. Files are intended to be processed locally.

This tool works with audio files already on your device. It does not provide audio recordings or circumvent DRM. Online identification and artwork features use third-party services.
