Privacy policy

Version date: 5 November 2026

In brief

Google Analytics is optional. It is not loaded until you choose “Accept analytics”. You can reject or change that choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

1. Who operates this site

NorVilTech is an independent brand operated from South Africa. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact support@norvil.co.za.

2. Information handled by this website

This static website has no account or checkout. The support form keeps the details you enter in your browser and opens your email app with a prepared message; the site does not send or store those details. Information you choose to email us is used to respond to your enquiry.

This website does not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, support messages, form fields, or content from documents or apps to Google Analytics. The analytics implementation sends page views and app-link clicks only. Page views use an allowlist of this site's page paths, excluding URL query strings and fragments, and do not send referrer URLs. App-link click events contain only a fixed app name and a fixed action (“try” or “open”); they do not contain the link URL. No custom event includes visitor input or personal contact details.

3. Optional Google Analytics

If you accept analytics, this site uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. It helps us understand which pages are visited and which app links are selected. Google may process technical information such as browser and device details, approximate location, and online identifiers. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including analytics identifiers, to distinguish visits and measure site usage.

The Google tag is not downloaded and analytics storage is not used unless you opt in. If you reject analytics, no Google Analytics tag is loaded. You can withdraw consent later from “Cookie settings”; this stops future analytics collection on this site and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to six months so we can remember it.

Google handles analytics data under its Privacy Policy and Data Processing Terms. Data retention is controlled by the Google Analytics property settings. Google may process data in countries outside your own, subject to Google's terms and safeguards.

4. Your choices and rights

You can choose “Reject analytics” in the consent notice, or open “Cookie settings” in the footer to change your choice. You can also use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Browser settings can be used to clear cookies and local storage, though clearing them also removes this site's saved consent choice.

Depending on your location, applicable laws such as POPIA or the GDPR may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information, or to object to processing. Contact support@norvil.co.za to make a request or raise a concern. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

5. Other services and changes

Links to the listed apps open services on separate domains. Those services have their own privacy practices and notices. This policy applies to norvil.co.za and its pages.

We may update this policy when our services or privacy practices change. The version date above identifies the current policy.

Questions about privacy? Email support@norvil.co.za.
